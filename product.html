Painted during the first rains of the 2024 season. The ink moved the way the sky felt: unrushed and inevitable. Rashmi worked on this piece across three consecutive mornings, letting the composition evolve without interference. The soft bloom of indigo at the lower third was entirely unplanned.

"I put the paper down and the rain did the rest."
