Ask Rashmi directly
Are all works originals? Are there prints available? +

All paintings listed on this site are originals: 1 of 1, signed and accompanied by a certificate of authenticity. A small number of limited-edition prints are available for select works. These are clearly marked and also numbered and signed.

How long does a commission take? +

Timelines vary by medium and scale. Alcohol ink works on Yupo Paper typically take 3-6 weeks once the brief is agreed. Large-scale acrylic commissions may take 8-12 weeks. Rashmi will give you a clear timeline before you commit, and progress updates are shared at agreed milestones.

Do you ship internationally? +

Yes. Works have been shipped to collectors in the UK, Singapore, the UAE, the USA, and across Europe. All international shipments are fully insured and professionally packed. Customs duties, if applicable, are the responsibility of the buyer. We guide you through the process and handle all documentation.

Can I see work in person before purchasing? +

Yes. Studio visits are available by appointment for collectors. Write to studio@rashmirao.com to arrange a visit. For collectors unable to visit in person, a detailed video walkthrough of the specific work can be arranged, showing the work in different lighting conditions and close-up texture detail.

What is the payment process? +

For available works: full payment is required before shipping. We accept bank transfer (India and international), Stripe (cards, international), and Razorpay (India). For commissions: a 50% deposit secures your slot and work begins once received. The remaining 50% is due before the work ships.

What does the certificate of authenticity include? +

Every original ships with a hand-signed certificate on studio letterhead that includes: the work's title, medium, dimensions, year, edition details (if applicable), a unique certificate number, and Rashmi's signature. The certificate is an important part of the work's provenance and should be kept with the piece.
