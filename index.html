Rashmi Rao

An internationally exhibited multidisciplinary artist who transforms emotion into visual language, working across alcohol ink, abstract acrylic, and graphic art to create works that breathe.

Her practice spans solo exhibitions, gallery showcases, and commercial collaborations across three continents. Her alcohol ink works are celebrated for their organic movement: controlled, yet entirely free.

Every piece begins with feeling, not planning. The canvas becomes a record of that moment.
