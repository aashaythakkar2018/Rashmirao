Our Story

Art That
Begins With
Feeling.

A practice of making things that outlast the moment they were made in.

Where Art
Becomes Story.

Amidst the richness of India's visual heritage and the restless energy of the contemporary art world, Rashmi Rao Designs sits at an unusual intersection: original paintings that breathe, wearable art that travels with you, and licensed collections that carry the same warmth into interior spaces.

Every work begins with a feeling, not a plan. The canvas, the Yupo paper, the silk: each becomes a record of a specific moment in the studio. That moment is then preserved and offered to collectors who want something genuinely alive on their walls.

The Journey

A Practice
Built Over
Years.

Driven by a lifelong immersion in art, colour, and cultural heritage, Rashmi Rao began her practice in 2014 with a commitment to making works that hold genuine emotional weight. Not decorative. Felt.

From early mixed-media experimentations in small formats to large-scale alcohol ink commissions spanning three continents, the practice has grown without losing its core instinct: every piece begins with feeling, not planning.

2014

Studio founded

Rashmi's first solo exhibition opened in Mumbai as the practice took its first quiet shape.

2016

Alcohol ink introduced

The Yupo Paper series began, giving the work a new immediacy and translucence.

2018

First international commission

A hotel installation in Singapore marked the practice's first global presentation.

2020

Wearable Art launched

Sarees printed from original paintings brought the studio's colour work into motion.

2022

India Art Fair feature

New Delhi press and exhibition coverage introduced the work to a wider contemporary audience.

2024

Monsoon Series

The largest alcohol ink works to date were created, expanding the scale and emotional intensity.

A subtle sequence of years and moments — a quiet connector between the artist's evolving intuition and the material experience of the work.

Philosophy

Beauty That
Earns Its
Place.

We have always believed in creating works that do not perform. They simply exist, confidently. Every decision in a painting, from the amount of ink to the moment of stopping, is made in service of that idea.

Craftsmanship, material honesty, and form are not afterthoughts. They are where a painting begins. The art of knowing when a work is finished, not overdone, is the hardest thing to teach.

01 Feeling over formula. No two works follow a predetermined outcome
02 Material integrity. Only archival-grade inks, pigments, and substrates
03 Originals only. No mass reproduction without artist authorisation
04 Every piece ships with a certificate of authenticity and provenance document
05 Direct collector relationship. No middlemen, no gallery markup

Every Piece
Has a
Reason.

The Rashmi Rao studio operates on a principle of intentional making. Each artwork is created without a production quota. A painting is done when it is done, not when the schedule says so.

The same care that governs the paintings extends to the wearable art line: sarees printed from original artworks, limited to 8 pieces per design, on fabrics selected for each work specifically.
